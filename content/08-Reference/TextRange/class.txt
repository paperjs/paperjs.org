<div class="reference-class">


<p>A part of a <a href="/reference/textstory"><tt>TextStory</tt></a>&rsquo;s content, from its <a href="/reference/textrange#start"><tt>start</tt></a>  to its <a href="/reference/textrange#end"><tt>end</tt></a>, whose style is that of its letters and paragraphs:  read, a property is the one they share, or <code>undefined</code>; set, it changes  them. It has the same style properties as items, e.g.  <code>text.words[0].fontSize = 24</code>. A range is a snapshot of two indices:  changing styles never moves it, but changing the content leaves ranges  taken before out of date, as indices into any string.</p>


</div>
